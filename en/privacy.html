1. Who is responsible?

Assurantie-onderzoek.nl is an activity of Blinqx (including Blinqx Verzekering & Hypotheek and the eBlinqx cloud platform), located at Hullenbergweg 109, 1101 CL Amsterdam, registered with the Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 84045132. Blinqx is the controller for the data you share with us through this website. For questions about privacy, please contact [privacy / DPO email address].

2. What data do we process through this website?

WhenWhat data
Requesting a quote or asking a questionName, organisation, job title, type of organisation, email address, telephone number (optional), the selected subject and the content of your message
Submitting a research questionYour research question, your role (optional), your email address (optional) and whether we may use your question in the Insurance Monitor
Subscribing to the Insurance MonitorName, organisation and email address
Emailing or calling us directlyThe data you choose to send or mention

Do not enter any data about customers, policyholders or other persons in the free text field. We do not need special categories of personal data, such as health data.

3. How do our forms work?

The forms on this website do not store anything and do not send anything to a server. When you click send, your own email program opens with a message to jeroen.oversteegen@blinqx.tech, with your details already filled in. We only receive your data once you send that message yourself. Until then, you can still change everything or discard the message.

4. For what purposes and on what legal basis?

PurposeLegal basis (GDPR)
Answering your question or quote request and preparing a quotePre-contractual steps taken at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)
Assessing your research question and, if you provide an email address, responding to itLegitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)
Including your research question, without name and organisation, in the Insurance MonitorConsent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by ticking the box and can withdraw at any time
Sending you the Insurance MonitorConsent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Every edition contains a link to unsubscribe.

We do not use your data for other purposes, and not for automated decision-making or profiling.

5. How long do we keep your data?

No longer than necessary for the purpose for which you provided it. We keep contact details and messages for a maximum of 2 years after the last contact, unless you become a client or a statutory retention obligation applies. We keep a subscription to the Insurance Monitor until you unsubscribe.

6. Data in our reports

Our reports contain only aggregated data that cannot be traced to individual customers, policyholders or advisers. Segments with fewer than 25 observations are not shown. The insurance intermediary or MGA is the controller for the customer file. The use of file data for aggregated market research will be laid down in an agreement with each participating party, with arrangements on the purpose, pseudonymisation before analysis, retention periods and a prohibition on re-identification. The intended legal basis is legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The agreements, the balancing test and the DPIA are in preparation; we will not process customer data for research before these have been completed. We do not use special categories of personal data or criminal-offence data. We do not carry out personal investigations into individual policyholders. See also our methodology.

7. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers who help us, for example with email and hosting, and only on the basis of a data processing agreement. We publish your research question only if you gave consent for this, and then without name and organisation.

8. Cookies and hosting

This website does not place cookies, does not use local storage in your browser and does not use tracking, analytics or marketing tools. No fonts or scripts from external parties are loaded.

The website is hosted via GitHub Pages. As with any web server, the hosting provider may record technical data, such as your IP address, for the security and operation of the service. GitHub, Inc. is based in the United States. Transfers of this data to the US are covered by appropriate safeguards, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. See the GitHub privacy statement.

9. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss and unlawful use. Only staff who handle your request have access to your message.

10. Your rights

You can access your data, have it corrected or have it erased. You can also object to the processing, have the processing restricted, have your data transferred or withdraw your consent. To do so, please contact [privacy / DPO email address]. We will respond within one month. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can lodge a complaint with the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Dutch Data Protection Authority).

11. Changes

We may amend this privacy statement, for example if our services change. The date of the latest version is shown at the top of this page.
