    1 Executive summary

    Your position at a glance

    Five key findings

    1. Your share of new policies rose from 7.2% in January to 8.6% in October, mainly driven by the home line. The peer group grew more slowly.
    2. You are quoted often, but in motor you lose out on premium. In 41% of the comparisons in which you were not chosen, you were in the most expensive quarter.
    3. After the renewal in July, the number of cancellations was 1.4 percentage points higher than in the market. The outflow mainly comes from customers who have been insured for more than five years.
    4. Your claims handling is faster than the market: a median of 9 working days from notification to settlement, against 13 in the market. When a loss adjuster is involved, this rises to 34 working days.
    5. After the storms in August, more climate-related damage was reported in the North and East than in the market. Your portfolio is over-represented there.

    17 Methodology & competition law

    How the figures are produced

    Source and coverage

    Quotes, applications, policies, policy changes and claim notifications from the intermediated channel, insofar as recorded in the software of the participating intermediaries and MGAs. Direct writers and online providers are out of scope.

    Calculation method

    Shares are weighted: the number per party divided by the total in the same period and line of business. Personal lines follow the ECOICOP v2 classification of CBS (Statistics Netherlands), commercial lines the SIVI classification.

    Privacy

    Aggregated figures only. We do not show segments with fewer than 25 observations. Intermediaries in the top 10 are pseudonymised; in a real report you only see the intermediaries you have a relationship with yourself.

    Competition law

    You see your own position against the market or an anonymous group of at least five parties. Premiums from public sources only, with a delay and as a range. No information about future premiums, commissions or acceptance policies of others.
