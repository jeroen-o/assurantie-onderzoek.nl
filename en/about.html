About us

The Blinqx research agency for the insurance sector

Market intelligence for insurers, MGAs and insurance intermediaries, with insight into quotes, policies and claims from the intermediary channel.

Who we are

Assurantie-onderzoek.nl is an activity of Blinqx, which supplies software including Blinqx Verzekering & Hypotheek and the eBlinqx cloud platform. Insurance intermediaries and MGAs use this software for advice, premium comparison, administration and claims handling. This lets us see what happens in the advice process: which insurers are compared, where applications end up, how portfolios develop and how claims are handled. Our research team turns this into management information.

Assurantie-onderzoek.nl is the sister site of hypotheekonderzoek.nl, which does the same for the mortgage market.

Our aim

Reliable market information on the insurance market, from the advice channel, so you can compare your position on a sound basis.

How we work

Independent

  • The research team works separately from the commercial departments of Blinqx
  • No client is given priority over, or influence on, the results of another
  • An independent advisory board will review our methodology and publications (in preparation, see governance)

Careful with data

  • Aggregated figures only, not traceable to customers or advisers
  • No medical or criminal-record data
  • Documented methodology and data quality checks

Fair competition

  • You see your own position, never figures of individual competitors
  • Comparison groups of at least five parties
  • Premiums only as a range and after the fact

Something in return for intermediaries

  • Intermediaries get insight into their own portfolio through the adviser subscription
  • Results of the adviser survey are shared with the participants
  • Market-wide trends in the public Insurance Monitor

Governance

Status. The agreements with participating intermediaries and MGAs, the balancing test, the DPIA and the governance below (advisory board, privacy officer, competition compliance officer) are in preparation. We will only deliver reports based on customer data once these are in place.

Assurantie-onderzoek.nl works with data from the software that Blinqx supplies to intermediaries and MGAs, and sells insights to insurers. That is why we have put firm arrangements in place for independence, the handling of data and competition.

Body or roleResponsibility
ManagementUltimately responsible for the research activity, the product range and compliance with this governance. Reports annually to the Blinqx management and to the advisory board.
Independent advisory boardThree external members with expertise in research methodology, insurance supervision and privacy or competition law. The board reviews new products and methods and assesses the Insurance Monitor and the methodology each year.
Research teamOnly the research team has access to the underlying, non-aggregated data. Access is granted per person, logged and reviewed every quarter.
Privacy officer (with the Blinqx DPO)Monitors the agreements with intermediaries and MGAs, the DPIA, the minimum cell size and the aggregation. Approves every new use of data in advance.
Competition compliance officerChecks every new product and every new report against the competition safeguards. Is the point of contact for clients with questions.

Standing arrangements

  • Use restriction. Clients may not use our insights for loyalty penalties, margin personalisation or steering and rewarding advisers. This is set out in the licence.
  • Separation of functions. Commercial teams of Blinqx have no access to client reports or to the underlying data.
  • No influence by clients. An insurer cannot influence the results, the comparison groups or the methodology, and has no access to reports of other clients.
  • No influence on advice. Our insights are not used to steer advisers in the software or in the premium comparison towards an insurer. There is no fee per application, policy or intermediary.
  • No personal investigations. We do not investigate individual policyholders and give no opinion on individual claims.
  • Transparency towards intermediaries. Participating intermediaries and MGAs know which data is used for research and for which products.
  • Complaints and escalation. Clients and intermediaries can report questions or complaints to the compliance officer. If we cannot resolve the matter together, it can be referred to the advisory board.
